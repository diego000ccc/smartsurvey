Connect Firebase database

To save real votes from everyone, SmartSurvey needs a free Firebase project. Follow these steps — it takes about 3 minutes:

1
Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with Google
2
Click "Add project" → give it any name → disable Google Analytics → click "Create project"
3
In the left menu click "Build" → "Realtime Database" → "Create database" → choose any location → select "Start in test mode" → click "Enable"
4
Copy the database URL shown at the top — it looks like https://your-project-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com
5
Paste it below and click Connect

The URL is saved in your browser. Firebase free tier supports up to 100 simultaneous connections.
